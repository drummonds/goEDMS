Search Syntax

SyntaxDescriptionExample
textFull-text search across document name and contentinvoice january
#tagInclude documents with this tag (AND logic for multiple)#Invoice #2024
~tagExclude documents with this tag~Draft
after:YYYY-MM-DDDocuments with document date on or after this dateafter:2024-01-01
before:YYYY-MM-DDDocuments with document date on or before this datebefore:2024-12-31
!untaggedShow only documents without any tags!untagged
!taggedShow only documents with at least one tag!tagged
*Show all documents*

Examples

cancer #InvoiceText search for "cancer" in documents tagged Invoice
#Invoice ~DraftInvoices that are not drafts
after:2024-01-01 before:2024-12-31Documents dated within 2024
#Invoice after:2024-06-01Invoices dated from June 2024 onwards
receipt before:2023-12-31Text search "receipt" with document date before 2024
#House-PurchaseAll documents in the "House Purchase" story

Date filters (before: / after:) apply to the document date field, not the ingestion date. Documents without a document date set will be excluded from date-filtered searches.
